# Stargate: Legacy
---

**A SCI-FI 3D RPG with top down view set in a galaxy hidden behind the Veil. Inspired by Stargate universe.**

## Lore
---

Two hundred years after a slave rebellion toppled the last of the Goa'uld, the victors built something worse. The **Concordate** — a militarized empire of former slaves turned wardens — now patrols the Veil, a pocket galaxy sealed off by the Ancients, dismantling or isolating any civilization that crosses its technological threshold. Earth's Stargate network is gone. The last human outpost drifts in the void between galaxies: the ark-ship *Legacy*.

You are a member of the *Legacy* expedition — a small crew of soldiers, scientists, and survivors searching for a way to restore the gate network and find allies among those the Concordate hasn't yet swallowed. What you find instead is a galaxy where nobody is innocent: a paranoid empire that fears its own past, free worlds that survive by looking away, and the ruins of the Ancients, who are watching. And waiting.

**Key features:**
- **The gate as ritual, not door.** Activating a Stargate requires power, symbol-matching, and noise — noise that draws attention. Every dial-out is a commitment.
- **Three layers of truth.** Dialogue branches across pragmatist, idealist, and *resonance* registers — the last unlocked only by skills and archetypes. NPCs remember deeds, not words.
- **A reputation matrix, not a morality meter.** Earn the trust of the Expedition, the Concordate, the Free Worlds, or the Silent — each with legitimate reasons to exist.
- **Archetypes, not classes.** Scout, Archaeologist, Jaffa Defector, Engineer, Diplomat — roles that change which solutions you can *see*, not just how hard you hit.
- **An archaeology of the present.** The Ancients left more than gates. Their broken infrastructure is the world's junk, its religion, and its last hope.

**Setting:** the Veil — a sealed galaxy of isolated worlds, decaying god-machines, and a fragile order built on fear.

**Tone:** grounded sci-fi, moral ambiguity, exploration over conquest.
